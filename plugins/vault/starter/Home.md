# Home

Everything you learn, filed by topic.

- **Add something:** write a note in `00 Inbox` (any device), tell Ari ("add to my notes on Docker: ..."), share a link or picture to "Save to my notes vault", or type `learn: ...` in Life Hub.
- **It gets filed:** within a few minutes it moves under `Topics/<Area>/`, linked to related notes. Your words are kept as you wrote them.
- **Make a note better:** put `#enhance` in it (or insert the "Mark for enhance" template). Claude rewrites it with sources and keeps your original text folded at the bottom.
