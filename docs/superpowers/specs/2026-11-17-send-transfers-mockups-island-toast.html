3. Helios: the side pop-up

Slides in at the bottom right of any Helios page while something is moving (on the phone, above the dock). It shows the newest transfer large, with any others as thin bars under it. When one finishes, a green "Saved" chip pops up with the rotating badge, then fades. Tapping the pop-up opens a Transfers list with the history.
